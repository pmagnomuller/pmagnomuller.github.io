Every personal site needs a first post. So here we are.

## Who I am

I'm a software engineer in Berlin. Most of my day job sits in energy and clean-tech — building systems that help people and grids behave a little more intelligently. Outside of work I keep a home lab, take study notes on books I actually finish, and tinker with AI tooling until something useful sticks. I ride, I lift when I can, and I read more than I publish.

## What this blog is

This is my personal writing space. No CMS, no editorial calendar — a Markdown file, a git push, and whatever I happen to be thinking about. The topics will follow my life: software craft, energy systems, homelab experiments, things I've learned, things I've broken, and the occasional note on what it's like to keep growing in this craft.

## What this blog is not

It is not a company channel. Views here are my own. Nothing I write represents the position of my employer or anyone I work with.

## Why now

I've had drafts sitting around longer than I care to admit — notes, half-finished guides, ideas that never left a private folder. Giving them a proper home felt overdue. The site is live, the pipeline works, and there is no longer a good excuse to keep writing into a void.

So: hello world. Let's see where this goes.
